Income tax is the tax paid on money you earn. These guides explain how it works in the United States and what's involved in filing a return.

## What this section covers

- **Income tax basics:** what counts as income and how the tax on it is worked out.
- **Filing:** what a tax return is and the steps involved in filing one.
- **Deductions and credits:** two ways to lower the tax you owe, and how they differ.
- **Tax-advantaged accounts:** accounts that get special tax treatment, such as some retirement and savings accounts.
