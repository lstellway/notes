A mortgage is a loan used to buy a home, with the home as security for the loan. These guides explain how mortgages work in the United States.

## Guides

- [How mortgage payments work](/finance/mortgages/how-mortgage-payments-work/): how the monthly payment is calculated, what else goes into it (taxes, insurance, and mortgage insurance), and how the rate and term change the total cost.
