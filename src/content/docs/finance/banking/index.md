Banks and credit unions offer accounts for spending money and for saving it. These guides explain how the common types work in the United States and how they differ.

## What this section covers

- **Checking accounts:** accounts for everyday spending, paying bills, and receiving pay.
- **Savings accounts:** accounts for setting money aside and earning interest on it.
- **Certificates of deposit (CDs):** deposits held for a set term, usually at a set interest rate.
- **Money market accounts:** savings accounts that can come with some checking features.
