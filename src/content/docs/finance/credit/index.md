Credit is money you borrow and agree to pay back later, usually with interest. These guides explain the common ways people borrow in the United States and how their borrowing history is tracked.

## What this section covers

- **Credit cards:** how card balances, interest, and payments work.
- **Credit scores and reports:** what a credit report records and how a score summarizes it.
- **Personal loans:** loans usually paid back in regular, equal payments over a set term.
