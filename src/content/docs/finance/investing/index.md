Investing means putting money into assets that can grow in value over time, with the risk that they lose value instead. These guides explain the accounts people in the United States invest through and the common things they invest in.

## What this section covers

- **Brokerage accounts:** general-purpose accounts for buying and selling investments.
- **Retirement accounts:** accounts meant for retirement savings, such as IRAs and 401(k)s.
- **Investment types:** what stocks, bonds, and funds are and how they differ.
